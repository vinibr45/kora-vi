# Local Discovery Basics

## Summary

Local discovery is how nearby buyers find, evaluate, and contact a business through channels such as Google Search, Google Maps, local landing pages, directories, reviews, social profiles, and WhatsApp.

## Core Idea

Local presence should make three things easy:

1. Find the business.
2. Trust the business.
3. Contact the business.

A local profile or page should clearly communicate category, area served, services, proof, location cues, opening hours when relevant, contact path, and next step.

## When To Use

- Improving a Google Business Profile.
- Creating local service pages.
- Reviewing a local business website or social profile.
- Diagnosing low contact volume from local search.
- Building WhatsApp-first contact flows.

## When Not To Use

- When the business is not location-based or local intent is irrelevant.
- When there is no capacity to respond to local leads.
- When the offer is unclear and should be fixed first.

## How To Apply

Check the local presence:

```text
Category: Is it obvious what the business does?
Area: Is the location or service area clear?
Offer: Are core services easy to understand?
Proof: Are reviews, photos, cases, or credentials visible?
Friction: Is contact fast and mobile-friendly?
Follow-up: What happens after WhatsApp, call, or form?
```

Prioritize fixes in this order:

1. Correct category and service clarity.
2. Strong contact path.
3. Trust signals.
4. Local keywords and pages.
5. Review generation and response process.

## Examples

Weak local CTA:

```text
Contact us.
```

Clear local CTA:

```text
Send a WhatsApp message to request a quote for your service in [city/area].
```

## Limitations

Local performance depends on competition, proximity, reviews, search demand, platform rules, response speed, and operational quality.
