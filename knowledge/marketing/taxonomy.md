# Marketing Taxonomy v0.2

This taxonomy guides how KORA should organize reusable marketing knowledge.

It should evolve only when useful. Do not create empty subfolders or documents without need.

## positioning

How a business, product, service, or person should be understood by a specific audience in relation to alternatives.

Possible entries:

- positioning concept;
- category design;
- audience segmentation;
- differentiation;
- value proposition;
- messaging hierarchy.

## offers

How value is packaged, explained, scoped, and presented to create a clear buying decision.

Possible entries:

- offer structure;
- offer clarity checklist;
- objections;
- proof;
- risk reversal;
- pricing communication.

## copywriting

How words are used to create clarity, trust, desire, and action.

Possible entries:

- headline principles;
- CTA patterns;
- landing page copy;
- sales page copy;
- Instagram captions;
- WhatsApp message structure.

## content

How recurring communication is planned and produced.

Possible entries:

- content pillars;
- weekly content calendar;
- educational content;
- authority content;
- offer content;
- storytelling;
- content repurposing.

## funnels

How attention, trust, conversion, and follow-up are structured across steps.

Possible entries:

- funnel stages;
- lead magnets;
- landing pages;
- follow-up flows;
- conversion paths.

## branding

How identity, perception, consistency, tone, and visual memory support trust.

Possible entries:

- brand voice;
- identity consistency;
- visual positioning;
- brand trust signals.

## local-presence

How a business appears and converts in local discovery contexts.

Possible entries:

- Google Business Profile;
- local SEO;
- reviews;
- local landing pages;
- WhatsApp contact flows.

## metrics

How marketing performance is measured and interpreted.

Possible entries:

- engagement metrics;
- conversion metrics;
- lead quality;
- channel performance;
- attribution limitations.

## research

How audience, market, competitors, platforms, and evidence are studied.

Possible entries:

- audience research;
- competitor research;
- voice-of-customer research;
- platform research;
- source reliability.

## frameworks

Reusable models that combine multiple marketing concepts.

Possible entries:

- content strategy framework;
- offer creation framework;
- landing page strategy framework;
- campaign planning framework.
