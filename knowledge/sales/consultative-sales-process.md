# Consultative Sales Process

## Summary

Consultative sales helps a buyer clarify their situation, evaluate fit, understand value, and choose the next step without being pushed into a mismatched purchase.

## Core Idea

A useful sales conversation moves through five stages:

1. Context: understand who the buyer is and what triggered the conversation.
2. Problem: identify the pain, cost, urgency, and desired outcome.
3. Fit: compare the buyer's need with the offer's true capabilities.
4. Decision: clarify objections, criteria, timeline, budget, and authority.
5. Next step: define the smallest clear action after the conversation.

Good sales work is not only persuasion. It is diagnosis, framing, trust, and decision support.

## When To Use

- Designing sales calls, WhatsApp flows, discovery forms, proposals, and follow-up messages.
- Diagnosing low conversion after leads arrive.
- Creating scripts for service businesses and consultative offers.

## When Not To Use

- When the offer is purely transactional and requires little diagnosis.
- When legal, compliance, or procurement rules define the sales process.
- When the business cannot honestly deliver the proposed outcome.

## How To Apply

Use this conversation skeleton:

```text
What made you look for this now?
What have you tried before?
What happens if this is not solved?
What would a good result look like?
How will you decide between options?
What needs to happen next?
```

Then convert answers into fit, recommendation, proposal, or disqualification.

## Examples

Weak sales behavior:

```text
Jumping directly to price before understanding context.
```

Better behavior:

```text
Diagnosing the buyer's problem, confirming fit, then explaining the recommended offer and next step.
```

## Limitations

Consultative sales requires honesty. It should not be used to manufacture urgency, hide poor fit, or pressure buyers.
